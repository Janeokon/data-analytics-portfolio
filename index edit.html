Graduate capstone project

Forecasting Uninsured Adults in Two Florida Counties

This project explored annual Small Area Health Insurance Estimates (SAHIE) for Miami-Dade and Bradford counties in Florida. Using R, I prepared and explored the data, examined time-series patterns, compared ARIMA/SARIMA model approaches, and developed forecasts as part of the original coursework.

RData cleaningExploratory analysisTime-series modelingForecasting
Research questionHow might uninsured-adult estimates change over time in the two counties?
Data sourceU.S. Census Bureau SAHIE, annual data from 2008–2022
ApproachData preparation, visualization, stationarity checks, and ARIMA/SARIMA model comparison
DeliverablesR Markdown analysis, written report, and capstone presentation

The project’s original forecast period was 2023–2027. Forecasts are presented as coursework outputs, not as verified current outcomes. Model conclusions will be checked against the report before final publication.

Red Wine Quality Analysis

Analyzed red wine physicochemical properties in R to explore their relationship with wine quality. Checked missing values, studied correlations, investigated regression assumptions, and compared linear regression with random forest models.

RR MarkdownExploratory analysisRegressionRandom forest
Linear regression · Model 9RMSE 0.6352 · R² 0.3915
Random forest · Model 9RMSE 0.5805 · R² 0.4982
Variables exploredAlcohol, volatile acidity, sulphates, and other measured properties
Data checksNo missing values reported; assessed normality and multicollinearity

Results are from the original academic report. The reported random forest Model 9 had lower RMSE and higher R² than linear regression Model 9. These results should not be interpreted as real-world production performance.

Caffeine Consumption: Survey Analysis & Biostatistics

Designed and analyzed a Google Forms survey to explore caffeine beverage choices, motivations, daily consumption, and self-reported sleep effects. The academic report presents descriptive statistics, prevalence calculations, comparisons by age and sex, conditional probability, and Bayes’ theorem.
